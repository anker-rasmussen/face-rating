How this works

A small face detector finds your face, straightens and crops it, and a neural network trained to predict human beauty ratings gives it a number from 1 to 10. Everything happens in your browser tab; the camera feed never leaves your device.

What the score means

It estimates the average taste of the people who rated the training photos (the SCUT-FBP5500 and MEBeauty datasets), nothing more. "Higher than X% of reference faces" compares you against a held-out set of those photos.

Known biases

  • The datasets cover a limited range of ethnicities, ages and styles. Scores for faces outside that range are less trustworthy.
  • SCUT-FBP5500's raters were mostly from China, so the model mirrors their preferences, not a universal standard.
  • Lighting, camera quality, angle and expression all move the score. That's why we warn you when the input looks off.

It's a fun statistical party trick, not an objective measure of worth, attractiveness or anything about who you are.
